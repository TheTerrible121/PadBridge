# Third-party notices

PadBridge's Windows release packages an unmodified FFmpeg executable from the
[BtbN FFmpeg Builds](https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds) project. The build
workflow selects its LGPL-labelled Windows x64 build. FFmpeg prints its exact
configuration and license with `ffmpeg.exe -L`.

PadBridge interoperates with, but does not bundle:

- Apple Devices / Apple Mobile Device Service on Windows.
- Virtual Display Driver by VirtualDrivers.
- SideStore or AltStore for free local iPad app signing.

Their respective licenses and terms remain with their maintainers.
